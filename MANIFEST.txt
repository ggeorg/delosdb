DelosDB heap insert lanes: qualification follow-up
==================================================

Purpose
-------
Close the evidence gaps in the per-writer heap insert lanes change (edf18924):
prove lanes actually engage, cover crash recovery, and make the attribution
probe reproducible from the repository.

Changes
-------
- FileContainer counts switches to insert lanes (one LongAdder increment per
  container switch, never on the per-row path) and exposes the count to tests.
- HeapInsertLaneTestSupport: test-only bridge compiled into the engine patch.
- HeapInsertLaneTest:
  - concurrent BARE/PK cases assert the table switched to insert lanes
    (both fail when lanes are disabled, so the assertion is meaningful);
  - the single-writer case asserts no switch happened;
  - new crash case: a separate JVM runs 8 concurrent writers on a PK table,
    commits and rolls back batches, leaves one batch per writer in flight and
    halts without shutdown; reboot must replay committed rows, undo in-flight
    and rolled-back rows, and pass SYSCS_CHECK_TABLE.
- tools/diagnostics/insert-probe: embedded F08 INSERT_100 probe, run script
  (Delos or upstream Derby, optional JFR, optional rare log switch) and a
  streaming JFR attribution script.

Deliberately unchanged
----------------------
- Insert-lane behavior, on-disk format, WAL, recovery

Qualification
-------------
- HeapInsertLaneTest: 4/4 pass; with -Ddelosdb.storage.insertLanes.disabled=true
  the two concurrent cases fail on the lane-switch assertion, as intended.
- delosStableTestSuiteStaticAnalysis, delosTestProvenanceStaticAnalysis,
  delosRepositoryIntegrityStaticAnalysis: pass.
