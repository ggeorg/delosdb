DelosDB cached page validity
============================

Purpose
-------
Stop every page fetch from taking the container's allocation-cache monitor.
FileContainer.getUserPage checked the page's allocation state under
synchronized(allocCache) before looking the page up. Every page allocation
invalidates the allocation cache, and revalidating it latches the
allocation page that the allocating thread holds, so one reader waited on
that latch inside the monitor while every other heap and B-tree page fetch
of the container queued on the monitor. With insert lanes, the held-intent
regrant and insert root routing in place, this was the largest writer wait
for PK inserts (7.7 s of 17.2 s writer blocking in a 2M-row, 8-writer JFR
recording) and the second largest for INDEXED (9.9 s of 34.1 s).

Changes
-------
- FileContainer.getUserPage: a page found in the page cache (findCached)
  skips pageValid and relies on the existing status check after latching.
  Deallocation changes the alloc map and the page's own status while the
  page is latched (deallocatePage), and an allocated page is latched by its
  allocator until it is initialized, so a reader that gets the latch sees
  the same answer pageValid would give. A page not in the cache still goes
  through pageValid before it is read, so pages past the end of the
  container are never read. In-place compress discards the container's
  cached pages after truncating.
- Allocation-cache revalidation moves from readers to the thread that
  changed it: BaseContainer.addPage calls allocationCompleted after its
  nested top transaction commits, and FileContainer.deallocatePagenum
  revalidates after releasing the allocation page
  (AllocationCache.validateIfInvalid). Page fetches used to revalidate the
  cache as a side effect, and AllocationCache.trackUnfilledPage drops
  updates while it is invalid; without the allocator-side revalidation
  inserts lose track of partly filled pages and allocate new ones
  (Derby3625Test, longRow). Queuing the dropped updates instead was tried
  and rejected: a stale "filled" hint applied after a page was reused hid
  an empty page (Derby3625Test, also when run alone).
- -Ddelosdb.storage.cachedPageValidity.disabled=true restores the old
  behaviour.
- CachedPageValidityTestSupport (raw-store test bridge) and
  CachedPageValidityTest: a deallocated page that is still cached is not
  returned by getPage or getPageNoWait, a page past the end is not returned,
  an allocated cached page is; and concurrent allocation, post-commit
  deallocation, page reuse and in-place compress against uncommitted-read
  lookups and scans, with contents, SYSCS_CHECK_TABLE and restart checked.

Deliberately unchanged
----------------------
- Allocation and deallocation themselves, allocation-cache invalidation
  points, unfilled-page tracking semantics, page status, the post-latch
  status and overflow checks, page cache, on-disk format, WAL, recovery
- The lock-free allocation-cache view (branch page-validity-fast-path)
  stays parked.

Qualification
-------------
- Embedded insert probe, 8 writers, rare log switch, 2M rows, 4 alternating
  pairs: PK 346k vs 317k rows/s (+9%, 4 of 4 pairs); INDEXED 161k vs 149k
  (+8%, 3 of 4); BARE 829k vs 670k (+24%, 4 of 4; one "off" run at 466k).
  Allocated pages after the probe are equal on and off (heap 12744; index
  counts within split variation).
- CachedPageValidityTest passes; without the post-latch status check the
  deallocated-page test fails (getPage returns the deallocated cached page)
  and passes again with the skip disabled.
- derbyStoreTests 435/435, including Derby3625Test and longRow, which caught
  the two rejected designs.
- delosRecoveryTests 57/57.
- delosConcurrencyTests 61: DEL-ISO-001, DEL-FK-004 and the task-only
  F08TransactionStatus test fail as on main.
