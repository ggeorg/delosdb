DelosDB F08 concurrent RawStore WAL causal prototype
Source authority: delosdb 89.zip
Baseline commit recorded in source archive: ceddd8189ca5380dc4328a16c83ad8c07c9c4374

Purpose
- Test the architecture-level hypothesis that Derby-family concurrent INSERT scaling is limited by coupling WAL ordering and WAL payload copy under the single LogToFile append authority.
- Preserve the existing RawStore WAL format, recovery, undo, checkpoint, and durability authority.

Experiment
- Default OFF: delosdb.experimental.rawStoreConcurrentLogAppend.enabled=false unless explicitly enabled.
- Ordinary prepared WAL records reserve disjoint current-buffer slices under LogToFile ordering, then patch/copy their frames after releasing the LogToFile monitor.
- FIRST/LAST transaction records and CLRs use a serialized prepared-record path.
- Flush, log-buffer/file transition, backward/forward scan-to-end, freeze, shutdown, and replication-role transitions quiesce outstanding concurrent reservations before crossing their inherited boundary.
- Oversized records and replication modes fall back to the serialized path.
- Existing on-disk WAL bytes and recovery record format are unchanged.

Causal evidence
- New benchmark task: :delosdb-tests:runDelosF08ConcurrentWalAppendCausal
- 16-byte PRIMARY_KEY_ONLY INSERT_100 at 1/2/4/8 clients, four runs.
- Delos Heap/Gen2 use 1 MiB RawStore log buffer + durability=test + concurrent WAL experiment.
- Upstream Derby and external engines remain unchanged.
- Captured Delos server logs must prove maxConcurrentCopies >= 2 for each Delos target or the benchmark task fails.
