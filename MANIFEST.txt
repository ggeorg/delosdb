DelosDB Gen2 transaction-status concurrent-commit qualification r1

Purpose:
- Qualify the concurrency prerequisite created by transaction-status hardening before any full F08 matrix.
- Measure overlap only after each transaction has successfully staged its durable status row.
- Require at least two status-backed commits to remain in the RawStore commit pipeline concurrently.

Production behavior:
- No locking, WAL, recovery, status-persistence, visibility, or publication semantics change.
- Diagnostic counters are disabled unless delosdb.diagnostic.mvccTransactionStatusCommit=true.
- Counters begin after status staging and end when RawStore invokes afterCommit/commit failure/abort cleanup.

Verification added:
- Eight independent PRIMARY KEY INSERT_100 transactions prepare before one commit barrier.
- Qualification requires entered=8, completed=8, active=0, and maxConcurrent>=2.
- Final row count verifies all 800 inserted rows committed.
