DelosDB RawStore concurrent mutation review
===========================================

Purpose
-------
Add the adversarial review of the RawStore concurrent mutation (RCM) design
and the record of the insert-path work that followed it, revisions 1 to 7.

Changes
-------
- docs/design/RAWSTORE-CONCURRENT-MUTATION-REVIEW.md: review of
  RAWSTORE-CONCURRENT-MUTATION.md and RCM-0/1 (sections A-N), the RCM-R0
  ledger specification and calibration review (O, P), JFR attribution (Q),
  insert lanes, the cross-engine source comparison and the held-intent
  regrant (R), insert root routing (S), and revision 7 (T): sane versus
  insane build profiles, cached page validity, the rejected WAL append
  window, the log buffer default, and the current plan.

Deliberately unchanged
----------------------
- Source code, tests, build

Qualification
-------------
- Documentation only; commit hashes and measurements cited in the document
  refer to commits on main and to the local experiment branches it names.
