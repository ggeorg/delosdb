DelosDB F08 B-tree key-prefix search r1

Purpose
-------
Reduce per-insert B-tree search work for unique indexes without changing
B-tree ordering, locking, page layout, WAL, recovery, split, or duplicate
semantics.

Changes
-------
- BTreeController reuses controller-local SearchParameters for inserts.
- BTreeController caches a FetchDescriptor for the unique user-key prefix.
- ControlRow page-search comparisons use that descriptor when supplied, so
  RawStore does not deserialize the trailing RowLocation on each binary-search
  probe for unique indexes.
- Non-unique indexes and all existing callers without a descriptor retain the
  inherited full-row fetch path.
