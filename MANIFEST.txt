DelosDB Gen2-C3 F05 read-head template reuse A/B overlay

Purpose
-------
Falsify whether the dominant allocation pressure remaining after the
read-directory-head-only experiment materially contributes to F05 matched-plan
latency.

Current evidence
----------------
Phase-2H baseline:
  Heap = 0.804483 ms/execution
  Gen2 = 1.520972 ms/execution

Read-directory-head-only ON:
  Heap = 0.767307 ms/execution
  Gen2 = 1.374313 ms/execution
  Gen2 improvement = ~9.6%
  ratio = 1.791087x

Current Gen2 allocation-by-site:
  DataValueFactoryImpl.getNullDVDWithUCS_BASICcollation = 78.23%
  directoryTemplate                              = 2.17%
  decodeDirectoryReadHead                        = 0.62%

Raw allocation stacks show the 78.23% site is entirely the six-field typed
template created by decodeDirectoryReadHead.

Experiment
----------
Requires the previous read-directory-head-only experiment to remain ON:
  -Ddelosdb.experimental.mvccReadDirectoryHeadOnly=true

Additional OFF-by-default switch:
  -Ddelosdb.experimental.mvccReuseReadDirectoryHeadTemplate=true

When both are enabled, the six-field typed directory read-head template is
reused per thread. Physical page fetch, kind/format checks, row identity,
version visibility, CURRENT/HISTORY semantics, mutation/commit paths,
WAL/recovery and durable formats are unchanged.

This is diagnostic only. Do not promote directly from this overlay.
