DelosDB F08-E commit-stamp elision upper-bound control

Purpose:
- Diagnostic-only causal upper bound for F08 fresh INSERT commit stamping.
- Adds a default-OFF property that deliberately skips commit-time CURRENT-row stamps
  only when the entire pending set is a fresh inline INSERT batch.
- The no-stamp lane is intentionally NON-SEMANTIC after commit: rows retain
  UNCOMMITTED_SEQUENCE and normal post-commit visibility is not valid.
- This is not a production candidate and must not be enabled outside the focused task.
